Projects

2026

Audio Map Explorer

Explore real city streets by sound and keyboard

  • A web app that lets blind and low-vision travellers explore real streets from OpenStreetMap by keyboard, with a visual map for sighted helpers.
  • Places are positioned in 3D audio around the listener, and every sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, including modelled car and motorbike engines running in an AudioWorklet.
  • A grid mode gives each kind of ground, such as road, building, park or water, its own sound. A phone mode offers a 7 by 7 touch grid that works with VoiceOver and TalkBack.
  • Speaks through the user’s screen reader using ARIA live regions, or through the browser’s built-in voice.

2026

NVDA add-ons

Extensions for the NVDA screen reader

  • Accessible Webpage: uses an LLM (Gemini, OpenAI or a local model) to rebuild an inaccessible web page as clean HTML with headings, landmarks and labelled fields, then repeats what the user does in that view on the real page.
  • Touch Explore Sounds: lets a laptop trackpad work like a touchscreen with NVDA, adding spatial earcons for items under the finger and split-tap activation.
